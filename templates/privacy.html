Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "our", or "the Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. When you use the Service, we collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect limited technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate the Service, including authenticating your account, storing your expense records, and generating summaries and spending breakdowns for you. We may also use your information to communicate with you about your account, respond to support requests, and improve the features and reliability of Spendly. We do not use your expense data to sell you products or share it with advertisers.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your account details and expense records are stored in our database and protected using reasonable technical and administrative safeguards. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You should avoid entering highly sensitive information — such as full financial account numbers or passwords — into free-text fields, as Spendly is not designed to store such data.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information or expense data to third parties. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate Spendly (such as hosting providers), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Spendly or its users.

5. Your Choices

You can review, update, or delete the expense records you have entered at any time through the application. If you would like your account and associated data removed entirely, you may contact us to request account deletion.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Service or for legal or operational reasons. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after any changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support channels listed in the application.
